🧠 Made By Brains Declaration

This is a declaration you can attach to any work to declare you haven't used any Generative AI whatsoever.

It's dedicated to the public domain under Creative Commons Zero, so you're free to use it in any way you like, for any purpose, with or without modifications and/or attribution.

Made By Brains Declaration
Version 1.0

Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) is any computer program that imitates a human by producing new content.

I recognise that GenAI poses significant risks to integrity, creativity, critical thinking, equality, privacy and the environment.

As such, to the extent of my knowledge, no GenAI was used for any purpose related to the this work's creation at any stage of this work's creation.

The entirety of this work was dreamt up, planned, crafted and polished by me, a human.
